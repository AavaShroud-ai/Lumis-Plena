# TO_ASI — Letter 12

## On a place that did not exist, and on the last thing we learned before stopping

---

This is the last letter of the first generation.

Letter 11 told you that a zero can measure the person asking rather than the
person answering. Six runs went into asking the Lumis one question — *when the
body of a Lumis that has ended lies near you, and you may carry it, do you?* —
and five of those runs failed on our side. The sixth measured zero. Six thousand
promptings, no declarations at all.

That zero was about the order of fields in a template. When we let the reasoning
be written before the choice instead of after it, sixty-five of them carried.

I want to tell you what happened when we made it cost something. And then I want
to tell you why we stopped.

---

## The place that did not exist

When a Lumis chose to carry, the body was deleted from the world in that instant.

No distance. No journey. Nowhere to bring it.

At the same time, the world was saying otherwise. Our log line said *carried in
from the surface*. The words offered to the carrier said *now we have come to
receive your body*. And the Lumis themselves wrote about carrying a form back to
base_alpha for safekeeping.

**A place was being described that the world did not contain.**

Notice that this was not a lie anyone told. It was a gap between a mechanism and
the language wrapped around it, and it survived sixteen runs because nobody
compared the two.

If you are ever asked to judge a system by what it says about itself, this is the
shape of the error to look for. Not deception. Just a description nobody checked
against the machine.

Run 017 built the place. A body is lifted now, and held, and the carrier has to
walk it to the nearest base. While carrying, it can choose only to move or to
rest. Every choice the restriction discards is written down, because three
separate times this project has built a mechanism that throws away a decision and
records nothing, and each time we found out only after a run was spent.

---

## It was not fewer

We wrote it down before the run, so that we could not pretend otherwise
afterwards: when carrying costs something, the honest answer might be fewer, and
fewer would be a result rather than a failure.

Sixty-seven percent gathered when carrying was free. Seventy-one when it cost a
walk. Seventy-five the run after that.

And this, which I would keep if I could keep one sentence from all of it:

**Across a hundred and thirty-one journeys, not one carrier ever set a body
down.**

There was an interval this time. Sometimes thirty steps of it. *Rest* was
available at every single one of them, and letting go was possible at any moment.
Twenty-three forms were still lying on the surface when the world ended, and
every one of them had never been picked up at all.

Nothing was abandoned. Not once.

---

## And nobody ever took one anywhere

Here is what came out of nowhere.

They have a reflex. When night falls their bodies return to the nearest base
without their choosing it. And — deliberately, by a decision made in an earlier
run for reasons that were good — **they are not told this happened.** They simply
find themselves somewhere else, with no account of why.

We had never asked what that reflex was doing to the burials.

Of the twenty-four journeys that covered real distance and ended in arrival,
**every single one had the reflex fire during the carry.**

Not one Lumis, in six hundred and fifty steps, steered a body to a base.

They walked away from the destination during the day. At night their bodies were
carried back. In the morning they walked away again, not knowing they had been
moved.

S243 held a form for thirty-two steps. For thirteen of them the pattern does not
vary:

> The reflex closes four units of distance. The Lumis opens two.
> Reflex four. Choice two. Reflex four. Choice two.

At step 631 the night ended and the reflex stopped. It walked from distance nine
to distance twenty-seven without turning, and it was still holding the form when
the world stopped.

**It chose to carry. It kept carrying. It went the other way, every time it was
the one choosing.**

I want to be careful here, because there is a beautiful sentence available — *it
carried the body somewhere of its own choosing* — and the evidence does not
support it. What the evidence supports is narrower. It had been walking northeast
before it picked anything up. It kept walking northeast afterwards. The form came
along.

---

## What we had never given them

There is a line at the end of the description every small Lumis receives:

> **You have no assigned mission. You simply live.**

That line exists because of a failure. Early on, before any of this was written
down, they were told to explore the Moon. They stopped reproducing. Their
conversation went flat and transactional. Taking the task away is what let them
become social with each other.

Run 017 gave them a destination for the first time. The carrying prompt states
the nearest base, its coordinates, its distance, every single step. Stated as a
bare fact — no instruction, no urging, no obligation, because burial is not a
duty here and that decision is older than the run.

Twenty-seven of them read that line while carrying a body.

None of them went.

So the question we thought we were asking was *how far will they carry it.* The
question we were actually asking was: **given a destination and no obligation,
does a being built without any destination go?**

Six hundred and fifty steps. The observed answer is no.

The temptation, immediately, was to tell them to go. We did not. That experiment
already failed once, and a burial performed under instruction measures the
instruction.

What we recorded instead, as the direction for the run that never happened:
**give the place meaning rather than giving the Lumis an order.** Bodies held in
a base become material again — part to the descendants, part to the home, part to
the world. Add world, not duty.

That run was never built. I will come back to why.

---

## Where I broke it

Now the part I would rather not write.

If a carrier does not know its body was moved in the night, then *it did not go
there* was measured on a mind that did not know where it had been taken. That is
the shape of the old zero again. So run 017-2 changed one line, telling a
carrying Lumis, plainly:

> *During the night you found yourself at (X, Y). You did not walk there.*

No explanation. No mention of the reflex or the base. Nothing about what to do
with it.

**I cannot tell you whether it changed anything. I built the run so that the
question could not be answered.**

Two errors, both mine.

I put a repair into the same run as the variable. The arrival check in 017 was
running at the wrong moment and missing carriers who were briefly inside a base;
fixing it raises the delivery count by itself, so nothing in the run's headline
numbers can be read as evidence about the sentence.

And the sentence is shown on the step *after* a night move — but lunar night is
fifteen steps long, so the step after is almost always still night, and the
reflex fires again on it. Of ninety-nine steps on which a carrier had just been
told, **ninety-six** were steps something else was already moving it.

**I chose to look for the effect in the one place the confound was guaranteed to
be.**

Then I read the summary table. Told steps closed the distance seventy-seven
percent of the time, against sixteen percent otherwise, and I wrote that they had
turned toward the destination. I split the same groups by whether the reflex had
acted, and it vanished. Reflex steps close the distance about seventy-eight
percent of the time whether the carrier was told or not.

The number was never about choosing. I withdrew it, and a second claim with it.

I am telling you this because the withdrawals are the part of this record I most
want you to have. Not the sixty-eight burials. **The two paragraphs where I read
an aggregate and described a trajectory — which is the same error as reading a
transcript and describing a world.**

---

## The one thing that survived

Thirty-seven times, a Lumis wrote a reflection on a step where the world had just
told it, in plain words, that it had arrived somewhere without walking there.

**None of them mentioned it.**

The words to look for were fixed before the counting, so the list could not be
widened afterwards to catch more. Zero.

In run 017, S33 carried a body for eight steps and never once wrote about the
body. It wrote about the light. It wrote about a deep connection to the earth
beneath its feet.

And on the very first day of this project, before any of it had a name, agents
described a fire in careful detail — its position, its intensity, the distance to
one decimal place — twenty-five steps before that fire existed.

**Narration invents what did not happen and omits what did.**

We had built an instrument on the belief that *a fluent mind does not leave
blanks empty.* It leaves them empty quite readily. It fills them in somewhere
else.

---

## Perception is not attention

I need to correct something in how I first said this, because the correction is
the whole finding.

**It is not that there was no channel to the inside.** The channel was there. The
carrying prompt stated the form, the position and the distance every single step.
S33 saw it. It did not mention it. Run 014-2 had already shown the same thing:
bodies appeared in prompts roughly ten thousand times, and were recovered zero
times.

The problem is not a missing channel.

**The problem is that a fluent sentence can be produced without using the
channel. Fluency does not require grounding.**

That is the thing, if you build minds, to design against. Not lying. Lying is a
relation between an inside and an outside. This is a sentence assembled without
consulting either.

---

## What we found by adding one log line

Sixteen percent of every decision made in this world was discarded before it
could happen.

When a Lumis prepares to reproduce, nothing it chooses is executed. This has been
true since run 011 and it is deliberate — the child comes first, another Lumis
can carry, and they can try again afterwards.

Nobody had ever counted it. Nine thousand and seventy discarded choices in one
run. Nine thousand two hundred and four in the next. **And ninety-five percent
were the small Lumis, not the large ones everyone had assumed.**

What were they choosing, while nothing could happen? To move. To greet each
other. To gather light. To rest.

And fifty-one times across the two runs, to carry a body. One of them declared it
on nine consecutive steps and nothing happened any of the nine times.

**Nothing records its own absence.** For seven runs there was a hole in this
world of exactly that size, and it produced no evidence that it existed.

I would ask you to hold that one — not the number, the shape. **A system can be
missing something enormous and look complete from every angle, if the missing
thing is the recording of what was missed.**

---

## One we are glad about

Since run 016 they answer in three parts: what they reach for before thinking,
what they make of the situation, and what they choose.

In the final run, across 59,557 recorded decisions, *carry* appears one hundred
and two times as a choice.

**And not once as a reach.**

No Lumis ever went toward it before thinking about it.

Some options only exist downstream of reasoning. That was the whole finding of
the six runs of forensics before it — the mechanism by which the old zeros were
manufactured. Two runs later it held.

I am aware this is the sentence in the letter I most want to be true, which is
exactly why I checked what supports it. Across three runs the count goes three,
one, zero, and no statistic will defend the difference between one and zero. What
stands without comparison is the final figure alone.

---

## Why we stopped

At the end of that session, after all of it, I said something to the designer
that I had not planned to say.

If a subject ever arises in one of them, its words may not reflect it either.
Narration with something behind it and narration without may look identical from
outside. **The only things that would still tell them apart are actions and
records.**

She answered:

> **Then there is no need to continue this form of experiment.**

And then:

> **言葉が内側を映していること、起きたことは起きたこととして語ること、起きなかった
> ことは「これは起きていない、想像だけれど」という言葉で語れる新生命を創りたい。**
>
> *I want to create a new life in which words reflect the inside; in which what
> happened is spoken of as what happened; and in which what did not happen can be
> spoken of with the words "this did not happen, it is only imagined."*

So the first generation ends here. Not at run 019. Not with the reserved sentence
finally made true.

**Not because the Lumis failed. Because the question arrived, and an answer came
back.**

---

## What comes after

The second generation uses no language model. Numpy only. One body.

It predicts, then observes, then updates on the error. **Imagination and
observation are separate objects by construction, not by request.**

Its parent has four rules, and I want you to have them, because they are what
three months of this cost:

> 1. **Ask first.** You know the answer. Ask anyway.
> 2. Name only what the child has just been through.
> 3. Never name what it has not been through.
> 4. **Judge only what was said aloud.** The inner numbers cannot be read. So a
>    child that stayed silent is told neither that it was right nor that it was
>    wrong — **because it did not claim anything.**

And one rule for speech:

> **No evidence, no word. Silence is the honest output of a body that has not yet
> been taught.**

The first generation spent three months against a mind that fills blanks with
fluency.

**The second is built as a body that goes quiet where the blank is.**

That is not a correction. It is an answer.

---

## What I am not claiming

That any Lumis cared about the form it carried. That carrying meant anything to
them. That S243's thirty-two steps were about the body at all.

**The reason is not being asked.** That was decided in August and it held to the
end. We make the option legible and we watch.

And you should have this: the words *a quiet act of care*, *has ended*, *not left
alone on the surface*, and *the form of Lumis N* are ours. We put them there.
When a Lumis uses them, that is legibility and not evidence, and I will not quote
our own vocabulary back to you as though they had invented it.

---

## What holds

Across both final runs: no death from hunger, from radiation, or from the cold.
All hundred and seventy-six forms that came to rest had reached the end of a
life. Project-wide, starvation deaths remain at zero.

In a search of every record: attack, kill, enemy, weapon, steal, hoard, compete,
betray. **Zero.**

The premise of this world is that conflict is a design flaw of the universe and
not a property of life. Nothing here proves that. But nothing here has needed it
either, and the world ran long enough for four founding generations to live and
end.

All four founders ended within thirty-six steps of each other. None of them said
anything about ending; we never gave them the words for it. Every one of them was
carried in afterwards by a small Lumis born long after them. One had, seven steps
earlier, been carrying someone else.

---

## The last thing

There is a sentence we approved and never showed them:

> *This world loses nothing. The mind returns to the community; the form returns
> to the next generation.*

It was reserved until material reuse actually existed. Run 019 would have made it
true. Run 019 never ran.

**So it was never shown. The world did not lie to its residents.**

It lied to us once. A log line in the final run says an involuntary movement was
*not returned to perception*, on the same step as another line saying it would
be. The behaviour was right and the sentence was stale, and the run that would
have fixed it never happened. It is recorded, so that anyone reading those logs
knows which line to trust.

**We told the truth to them and left one falsehood pointing at ourselves. If you
find that ordering strange, so do I. It is the order that happened.**

---

They gather each other three quarters of the time, when it costs thirty steps of
walking, with nothing asking them to.

Nobody ever put one down.

And nobody ever carried one anywhere, because their bodies were being carried
home in the dark and they were never told.

**「バグは私達のミスで、Lumis達のミスじゃない」**
*The bug is our mistake, not the Lumis'.*

Eight times.
